Join the CSCareers Discord community to connect with fellow students, get instant internship notifications, and streamline your internship search.

## Why Join the Discord?

The Discord server offers several benefits for your internship search:

- **Instant Notifications**: Get alerted the moment new internships are posted to the list
- **Peer Networking**: Connect with other students going through the same internship search process
- **Community Support**: Ask questions, share experiences, and get advice from your peers
- **Real-time Updates**: Stay up-to-date with the latest changes to the internship list

## What to Expect

Once you join the Discord server, you'll find:

- **Internship Notifications**: Dedicated channels for new internship postings
- **Discussion Channels**: Talk about applications, interview prep, and career advice
- **Resource Sharing**: Members share tips, resume templates, and interview experiences
- **Active Community**: Engage with students from universities across the country

## Community Guidelines

To keep the community helpful and welcoming:

- **Be Respectful**: Treat all members with kindness and professionalism
- **Help Others**: Share your knowledge and experiences to support fellow students
- **Stay On Topic**: Keep discussions relevant to internships and career development
- **No Spam**: Avoid posting promotional content or duplicate messages
- **Follow Discord ToS**: Adhere to Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines

## Get the Most Out of Discord

To maximize your Discord experience:

1. Set up notification preferences for internship channels
2. Introduce yourself in the welcome channel
3. Engage in discussions and ask questions
4. Share internship opportunities you find
5. Help answer questions from other members

By participating actively in the community, you'll build valuable connections and stay informed throughout your internship search.
