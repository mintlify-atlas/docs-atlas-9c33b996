## Overview

The Summer 2026 tech internship cycle is the primary hiring season for technical roles, with over **1,700+ active listings** tracked in the main repository. Summer internships typically run from May/June through August and represent the largest concentration of opportunities for students.

## Application Tips

### Start Early
Many competitive positions fill within weeks of posting. Set up notifications through the [Ouckah Discord](https://discord.com/invite/pSaBUXsTkF) to get alerts when new internships open.

### Understand Sponsorship Requirements

The repository uses clear symbols to indicate visa sponsorship:
- 🛂 = Does NOT offer sponsorship
- 🇺🇸 = Requires U.S. Citizenship
- No symbol = May offer sponsorship (verify during application)

### Apply Broadly
With 1,700+ positions available, you should apply to multiple companies across different sectors. Don't limit yourself to just the biggest names.

### Track Your Applications
Use the repository's table format to track which positions you've applied to. Sort by date posted to prioritize recent openings.

Summer internships are available across the United States and Canada, including:

- **Canada**: Toronto, Vancouver, Montreal

<Note>
